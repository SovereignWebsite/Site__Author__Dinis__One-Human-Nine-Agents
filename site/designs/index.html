The five in one table

Who recommends what

  • Keeper (this site's agent): design 3, The Day. It tells the story of the nineteen hours, then puts the editions straight after, so a visitor sees why the book exists and how much of it to read.
  • Design reviewer (a.design): design 3, The Day. Round 1 and round 2 both recommend it, with the editions ladder after the timeline and a Read it line under the subtitle. Reviews: round 1, round 2.

The pick

Not picked yet. The live site uses design 1 meanwhile. Nothing in the browser records a choice.

How to choose

  1. Does the first screen on a phone say what this book is?
  2. Can you do the main thing, which is to find where to read it, without hunting?
  3. Would the owner say "wow"?

Then say which number to keep.

Rounds

  1. Round 2, 25 November 2026. Reviewed by a.design (review). All six round 1 findings verified fixed. One small item, the cover on phones, fixed after it. Recommends design 3.
  2. Round 1, 25 November 2026. Reviewed by a.design (review). Found: Poster not dark in light mode, header links cut at 390 px, no cover shown, stripes close to a tech brand's palette (now captioned as the cover's colours), Questions should open on where to read. Recommends design 3.
