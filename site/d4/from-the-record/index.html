The book is written in public. These are the pages on its own site where the decisions, sources and updates are kept. We link them and do not copy them. Checked 25 November 2026.

Where the book's own pages differ

  • The book's home page says nine agents were staffed on the day and a tenth, security, was proposed and never staffed (C011). Its manuscript page describes "a ten-agent team". This site uses nine, the number the home page and the decisions page give.
  • The home page gives 18 h 44 min, from the first commit at 00:16 to the talk at 19:00, and calls it the nineteen hours of the title. The decisions page says "about nineteen hours" (C010). Both are the same span; the title rounds it.
  • The team and updates pages carry dates from early to mid November 2026, so their status lines may be behind the book's home page.
